About alchemy

We are a creative video production & post-production studio

Who we are

ALCHEMY is a family-owned creative video production and post-production company based in the San Francisco Bay Area. Small by design so we can stay hands-on, move quickly, and scale the right team around each project.

For more than 16 years, we’ve partnered with global brands and startups across technology, food, apparel and beyond. Specializing in brand campaigns, commercial production and branded content, we take projects from concept and creative development through production, post-production to final delivery.

Research. Concept. Create.

01

Creative Development

Before we pick up a camera, we listen. We dig into your brand, your goals, and your audience to understand what you actually need. Starting from your brief or a blank page, we ask the right questions, then shape the answers into a clear creative vision, from first ideas to a locked treatment and script.

02

Video Production

Once the concept is set, we handle every part of production — casting, crews, locations, gear, schedules, talent, travel, art direction, props, wardrobe and all the logistics in between. We often pair photography with our video shoots, giving brands a cohesive library of still and motion assets from the same production. From a one-day Bay Area shoot to a multi-city production across the U.S. or abroad, we keep every moving part on track.

03

Post-production

This is where the story comes to life. We shape the narrative in the edit, then bring it home with motion design, animation, sound, music and color — all the details that give the final film its polish, mood and impact.
