Questions we get asked a lot

Commercials, branded content, social-first video, documentary-style content, and animated content — basically anything that needs to look great and get a message across. We're especially great at telling human stories inspired by real experiences.

Editing, color grading, sound design, and motion graphics — the full post-production process. Editorial is in our DNA: our lead creative came up through the edit bay, so story, pacing, music, and structure are part of how we think from the very beginning, not something we figure out at the end.

Alchemy offers full-service creative video production, from start to finish: concept, shoot, edit, deliver. We'll take on just one piece too, if it's the right fit.

Absolutely. Our founder started as an editor, so we know exactly what a good edit needs, even when it's buried in raw footage. Send it over and we'll find the story in it.

We work with startups, Fortune 500 brands, and companies of all sizes in between. Being in the San Francisco Bay Area, we naturally work a lot in tech, but also, lifestyle, food, and plenty outside those categories.

More than anything, we like working with good people who communicate well, are willing to take some risks, trust us to do what they hired us to do, and have a realistic budget to do the work well. If you don't see your industry listed, don't let that stop you from reaching out. We're always open to interesting work in new spaces.

Absolutely. While we often work directly with brands, we love collaborating with agencies and other creative teams. We can partner on creative development from the start, which is our favorite, or come in specifically for production, post-production, or both.

We're based just outside San Francisco, but we don't stay put. We've got clients on both coasts and in between, and we've filmed all over the world, including the U.S., Canada, South America, Mexico, Europe, Australia, Japan, India,and Southeast Asia. If there's a story worth telling, we'll find a way to get there.

Every project is different (you’ll see this a lot in this section!), but the general flow is similar: discovery, kickoff, creative development, pre-production, production, post-production, revisions, and final delivery. We keep things clear along the way, so you always know what's happening, what we need from you, and where the project stands. And if anything comes up that could affect the budget, you'll know about it before we move forward. No surprise overages at the end.

Timelines depend on what we're making:

  • Animation: 4-6 weeks from concept to delivery
  • Explainer or social-first video: faster turnaround, often on the shorter end of that range
  • Full-service live action (creative development, filming, and post-production): 8-12 weeks
  • Larger campaigns with multiple deliverables or custom animation: longer, depending on scope.12 weeks +

The real answer always depends on scope and complexity. Need it faster? We can make that happen too. Rush fees are a thing but so is a good deadline.

The answer nobody loves: it depends. Every project is different, so we price based on scope, complexity, and what it'll take to bring it to life well. If you have a budget in mind, tell us. Knowing where we need to land helps us build the smartest creative and production approach around it, without sacrificing quality. Nobody likes a guessing game.
